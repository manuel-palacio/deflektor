DEFLEKTOR

Bend the light. Burn the cells. Don't overload.

Rotate the mirrors to steer the laser into every cell. When they're all gone, the barriers around the receiver vanish: guide the beam into it to clear the level.

Purple bricks reflect the beam, blue bricks absorb it. Prisms scatter it at random, and rotating polarisers only let it through when they line up. Avoid mines and don't reflect the beam back into the laser, or the overload meter climbs. Energy drains all the time, so be quick.

  • Click a mirror to turn it clockwise, right-click to turn it back, or hold to spin it. The mouse wheel works too. Hovering shows where the next turn would send the beam.
  • Touch: tap near a mirror to select it, then turn it with the big ↺ ↻ buttons (hold to keep turning), drag around it like a dial, or tap its right / left half. ‹ › pick the next mirror.
  • Finding it hard? Pick Relaxed in Settings for more energy and a slower overload.
  • Arrows / WASD jump between mirrors. Space / X rotates, Z rotates back.
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, H hint, R restart, P / Esc pause, M mute.
  • Stars: ★ solve it, ★★ within the turn limit, ★★★ within the turn and time limits without restarts or hints.
